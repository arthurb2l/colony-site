Odd and even numbers

A whole number is even if it can be split into two equal whole-number halves, or equivalently if it is a multiple of 2. A whole number is odd if it leaves 1 when divided by 2.

Even numbers: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ...
Odd numbers: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, ...

The last digit tells you

Look at the last digit only. Numbers ending in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8 are even. Numbers ending in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9 are odd. For example, 348 is even because it ends in 8, and 1,205 is odd because it ends in 5.

Multiplying always gives an even answer if at least one number is even. Adding two odd numbers always gives an even answer, so the sum of two odd numbers is never odd.

Why it matters

Checking parity (odd or even) is a quick way to catch mistakes. If you add up a list of even numbers and get an odd total, something went wrong. Parity also helps with puzzles: if a pile of counters splits into two equal teams with none left over, the total must be even.

Try it

  1. Is 2,468 odd or even?
  2. Is 57 odd or even?
  3. What is 9 + 13? Is it odd or even?
  4. Is 7 x 8 odd or even?
  5. Is 5 x 5 x 5 odd or even?

Answers

  1. Even (ends in 8).
  2. Odd (ends in 7).
  3. 22, even (odd + odd).
  4. Even (one factor, 8, is even).
  5. Odd (all factors are odd).
