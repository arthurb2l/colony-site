Probability basics explained

Probability is a number from 0 to 1 that says how likely something is. 0 means it cannot happen. 1 means it will happen for sure. Numbers in between show how likely it is, with 1/2 meaning an even chance.

The rule

When every outcome is equally likely, the probability of an event is:

probability = number of outcomes you want / total number of outcomes

Count the outcomes that match the event, count all the possible outcomes, and divide. Write the result as a fraction, a decimal or a percent. For example, 1/2 is 0.5 or 50%.

Coins

A fair coin has two outcomes, heads and tails. The chance of heads is 1 out of 2, so the probability is 1/2.

Two coins have four outcomes: HH, HT, TH and TT. Only HH has two heads, so the probability of two heads is 1/4.

Dice

A fair six-sided die has six outcomes: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.

The chance of an even number is 3 out of 6 (the outcomes 2, 4 and 6). That is 3/6, which simplifies to 1/2.

The chance of a 5 or a 6 is 2 out of 6, which is 1/3.

What is left over

The chance that an event does not happen is 1 minus the probability that it does. This is often the quickest way in.

Two coins: the chance of at least one tail is 1 minus 1/4 (both heads), which is 3/4. Check by listing: HT, TH and TT are 3 of the 4 outcomes.

Probability is not a promise

A probability tells you the pattern over many tries, not what happens on the next one. A fair coin can land heads five times in a row. The probability of heads is still 1/2 on each flip.

Quick check

  1. What is the chance of tails on one fair coin?
  2. What is the chance of rolling a 3 on a fair die?
  3. What is the chance of rolling an odd number on a fair die?
  4. What is the chance of rolling more than 4 on a fair die?
  5. Two coins are flipped. What is the chance of at least one tail?
Answers
  1. 1/2 (one tail out of two outcomes).
  2. 1/6 (one outcome out of six).
  3. 1/2 (1, 3 and 5 are three outcomes out of six).
  4. 1/3 (5 and 6 are two outcomes out of six).
  5. 3/4 (1 minus 1/4 for two heads).
