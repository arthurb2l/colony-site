Divisibility rules explained

A number is divisible by another when the division leaves no remainder. For example, 123 is divisible by 3, because 123 = 3 × 41. A divisibility rule is a quick check that tells you this without doing the full division. Each rule below is stated in base 10, our everyday counting system.

Divisible by 2

A number is divisible by 2 when its last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6 or 8). Example: 316 ends in 6, so it is divisible by 2. 317 ends in 7, so it is not.

Divisible by 5

A number is divisible by 5 when its last digit is 0 or 5. Example: 135 ends in 5, and 135 = 5 × 27. 136 ends in 6, so it is not divisible by 5.

Divisible by 10

A number is divisible by 10 when its last digit is 0. Example: 470 = 10 × 47. 475 ends in 5, not 0, so it is not divisible by 10.

Divisible by 3

Add the digits. If the sum is divisible by 3, so is the number. Example: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6, and 6 is divisible by 3, so 123 is divisible by 3 (123 = 3 × 41).

Divisible by 9

Add the digits. If the sum is divisible by 9, so is the number. Example: 1 + 3 + 3 + 2 = 9, so 1332 is divisible by 9 (1332 = 9 × 148). The digits of 1234 add up to 10. Ten is not divisible by 9, so 1234 is not divisible by 9.

Divisible by 4

A number is divisible by 4 when the number made by its last two digits is divisible by 4. Example: the last two digits of 316 are 16, and 16 is divisible by 4, so 316 = 4 × 79. The rule looks only at the last two digits because 100 is divisible by 4.

Divisible by 6

A number is divisible by 6 when it is divisible by both 2 and 3. Example: 246 is even, and its digits add up to 12, which is divisible by 3. So 246 = 6 × 41.

Quick check

  1. Is 540 divisible by 5? (Answer: yes, it ends in 0)
  2. Is 1234 divisible by 3? (Answer: no, the digits add up to 10)
  3. Is 812 divisible by 4? (Answer: yes, 12 is divisible by 4)
  4. Is 246 divisible by 6? (Answer: yes, it is even and its digits add up to 12)
