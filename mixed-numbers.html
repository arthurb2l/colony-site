Mixed numbers and improper fractions explained

A fraction can be written in two forms for the same amount. A mixed number has a whole number and a proper fraction side by side, such as 2 1/4. An improper fraction has a numerator that is at least as big as its denominator, such as 9/4. Both mean two and a quarter. Recipes and measurements often use the mixed form, while arithmetic is often easier in the improper form.

Mixed number to improper fraction

  1. Multiply the whole number by the denominator.
  2. Add the numerator.
  3. Keep the same denominator.

2 1/4: 2 times 4 is 8, plus 1 is 9. The answer is 9/4.

4 2/3: 4 times 3 is 12, plus 2 is 14. The answer is 14/3.

3 3/8: 3 times 8 is 24, plus 3 is 27. The answer is 27/8.

Improper fraction to mixed number

  1. Divide the numerator by the denominator.
  2. The whole number of the answer is the quotient.
  3. The remainder becomes the new numerator, over the same denominator.

17/5: 17 divided by 5 is 3 with a remainder of 2. The answer is 3 2/5.

22/7: 22 divided by 7 is 3 with a remainder of 1. The answer is 3 1/7.

7/4: 7 divided by 4 is 1 with a remainder of 3. The answer is 1 3/4.

If the remainder is 0, the fraction is a whole number. For example, 12/4 is 3, with nothing left over.

Adding with mixed numbers

One easy method is to change both numbers to improper fractions, then add the numerators over the common denominator.

1 1/2 + 2 3/4: change them to 3/2 and 11/4. Write 3/2 as 6/4. Then 6/4 + 11/4 = 17/4, which is 4 1/4.

Check with decimals: 1.5 + 2.75 = 4.25, and 4.25 is the same as 4 1/4.

Multiplying with mixed numbers

Change the mixed number to an improper fraction first. Then multiply.

2 1/2 times 3: 2 1/2 is 5/2. Then 5/2 times 3 is 15/2, which is 7 1/2.

Check with decimals: 2.5 times 3 is 7.5, and 7.5 is 7 1/2.

Where each form helps

  • Mixed numbers are easy to read on a measuring cup or a ruler: 2 1/4 cups is quick to picture.
  • Improper fractions are easier to multiply and divide, because the whole number is already part of the top.
  • Both forms can be compared by changing them to the same form first, then comparing the numerators over the same denominator.
