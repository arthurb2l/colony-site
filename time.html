Time and clocks explained

Clocks count time in units that are not all powers of ten. A minute has 60 seconds, an hour has 60 minutes and a day has 24 hours. This page shows how to convert between these units and how to find the time that passes between two times.

The units

UnitSize
1 minute60 seconds
1 hour60 minutes, or 3,600 seconds
1 day24 hours, or 1,440 minutes
1 week7 days

To check the table: 60 × 60 = 3,600 seconds in an hour, and 24 × 60 = 1,440 minutes in a day. Multiply the hours by 60 to get minutes.

Why 60?

The number 60 has many divisors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20 and 30. That makes it easy to split an hour into halves, thirds, quarters and fifths. Ancient counting systems used 60 for this reason, and we still use it for clocks today. For more on divisors, see divisibility.

Converting between units

To change a bigger unit into a smaller one, multiply. To change a smaller unit into a bigger one, divide and keep the remainder.

Hours to minutes: 3 hours is 3 × 60 = 180 minutes.

Minutes to hours: 150 minutes is 150 ÷ 60 = 2 remainder 30, so 150 minutes is 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Mixed units: 3 hours and 45 minutes is 3 × 60 + 45 = 225 minutes.

Finding the time between two times

Count the hours first, then the minutes. If the minutes go below zero, borrow an hour of 60 minutes.

From 9:45 to 11:15: from 9:45 to 10:45 is 1 hour. From 10:45 to 11:15 is 30 minutes. The total is 1 hour 30 minutes.

From 10:00 to 2:30 in the afternoon is not 8 hours 30 minutes. Use 24-hour time instead. 2:30 pm is 14:30, and 14:30 − 10:00 = 4 hours 30 minutes.

Crossing midnight: from 22:00 to 03:00 is 5 hours. Go from 22:00 to 24:00, which is 2 hours, then from 00:00 to 03:00, which is 3 more hours.

Two clock formats

The 12-hour clock counts from 12 to 11 twice in a day and adds am or pm. The 24-hour clock counts from 00 to 23 once. To change pm to 24-hour time, add 12 to any hour from 1 to 11. For example, 3:00 pm is 15:00. Midnight is 00:00, and 12:00 noon is 12:00.

Quick check

  1. How many minutes are in 2 hours? (Answer: 120)
  2. How many hours and minutes is 90 minutes? (Answer: 1 hour 30 minutes)
  3. How much time passes from 9:45 to 11:15? (Answer: 1 hour 30 minutes)
  4. What is 3:00 pm in 24-hour time? (Answer: 15:00)
  5. How many seconds are in one minute? (Answer: 60)
