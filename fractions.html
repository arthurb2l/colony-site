Fractions explained

A fraction names a part of a whole. It is written with two numbers and a line between them, like 3/4. The number below the line is the denominator, and it says how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The number above the line is the numerator, and it says how many of those parts you have.

So 3/4 means three parts out of four equal parts. A pizza cut into 4 slices with 3 slices left has 3/4 of the pizza left.

Equal parts

For the fraction to mean what it says, the parts have to be the same size. A circle cut into 2 equal halves gives 1/2 for each half. Two halves make the whole, so 2/2 = 1.

A fraction with a numerator smaller than its denominator is less than one whole. 5/8 is less than 1. A fraction with a numerator equal to or larger than its denominator is one whole or more. 5/4 is one whole plus 1/4, which can also be written 1 1/4.

Equivalent fractions

Different fractions can name the same amount. Cut a bar into 2 equal parts and take 1, or cut it into 4 parts and take 2. Both show half the bar:

1/22/43/64/8

To make an equivalent fraction, multiply the top and the bottom by the same number. 1/2 times 3 over 3 gives 3/6. The amount does not change, because multiplying by 3/3 is the same as multiplying by 1.

Simplifying

To simplify a fraction, divide the top and the bottom by the same number until no number divides both evenly.

Take 6/8. Both 6 and 8 divide by 2, so 6/8 = 3/4. Neither 3 nor 4 divides by 2 or by any other whole number greater than 1, so 3/4 is in simplest form.

Take 12/18. Both divide by 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3. So 12/18 = 2/3.

Comparing fractions

When two fractions have the same denominator, the larger numerator is the larger fraction. 5/8 is more than 3/8, because five eighths is more pieces of the same size than three eighths.

When the denominators differ, make them the same first. Compare 3/4 and 2/3. Multiply to get 9/12 and 8/12, using 4 × 3 = 12 and 3 × 4 = 12 for the bottoms. Now 9/12 is larger than 8/12, so 3/4 is larger than 2/3.

Adding fractions with the same denominator

When the denominators match, add the numerators and keep the denominator the same. The parts are the same size, so you are only counting them.

1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4. Three slices of a pizza cut in 4 make 3/4 of the pizza.

3/8 + 4/8 = 7/8. The denominator stays 8, because the pieces are still eighths.

A fraction of a number

To find 3/4 of 20, first find 1/4 of 20 by dividing by 4: 20 ÷ 4 = 5. Then multiply by the numerator: 5 × 3 = 15. So 3/4 of 20 is 15.

Fractions and percentages

A percent is a fraction with a denominator of 100. So 25/100 = 25%. Using the same idea, 1/2 = 50%, 1/4 = 25%, and 3/4 = 75%. For more on percents, see the percentages page.

Quick check

Try these by hand, then compare with the answers below.

  1. Simplify 10/20.
  2. Which is larger, 5/6 or 4/6?
  3. Add 2/5 and 1/5.
  4. What is 1/2 of 18?

Answers: 1) 1/2 (divide by 10). 2) 5/6 (same denominator, larger numerator). 3) 3/5. 4) 9 (18 ÷ 2 = 9).
