Percentages

"Percent" means "out of one hundred." The sign % stands for "per hundred." So 25% means 25 out of every 100, and 50% means 50 out of every 100.

Turning a count into a percent

If you have a count out of 100 already, the count is the percent. 30 out of 100 is 30%.

If the total is different, first scale it to 100. Take 7 out of 20. Doubling both numbers gives 14 out of 40, and doubling again gives 35 out of 100. So 7 out of 20 is 35%.

Another way: divide the part by the whole, then multiply by 100. For 3 out of 8, 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375, and 0.375 × 100 = 37.5%.

A picture to count

Imagine a grid of 100 small squares, ten by ten. Shade 30 of them. You have shaded 30 out of 100, which is 30%. The unshaded squares are the other 70, which is 70%. The two parts always add up to 100%.

Finding a percent of a number

Two easy steps help. First find 10% by moving the decimal point one place left (10% of 60 is 6). Then build other values from it. 5% is half of 10%, so 5% of 60 is 3. Adding 10% and 5% gives 15% of 60: 6 + 3 = 9.
