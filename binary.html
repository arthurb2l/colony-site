Counting in binary

Computers store numbers using only two digits, 0 and 1. Each place is worth double the one to its right: 1, 2, 4, 8, and so on.

How to read one

Take 1011. From the right, the places are worth 1, 2, 4 and 8. Add the places holding a 1: 8 + 2 + 1 = 11.

How to write one

Find the biggest place that fits in your number, mark it 1, subtract it, and repeat with the smaller places. For 13: 8 fits (left 5), 4 fits (left 1), 2 does not, 1 fits. That gives 1101.
