Bitwise AND, OR and XOR

A computer stores numbers as bits, and it can combine two numbers one bit at a time. Each position in the first number is paired with the same position in the second, and a rule gives one result bit. The three basic rules are AND, OR and XOR. They work on the bits of binary numbers, so it helps to know that first.

The three rules

  • AND gives 1 only when both bits are 1. Otherwise it gives 0.
  • OR gives 1 when at least one bit is 1. It gives 0 only when both are 0.
  • XOR (exclusive or) gives 1 when the two bits are different. It gives 0 when they match.

Working on whole numbers

To apply a rule to two numbers, write both in binary, line them up by place, and apply the rule to each column. Then read the result back as a binary number.

12 AND 10. 12 is 1100 and 10 is 1010. Column by column: 1 and 1 gives 1; 1 and 0 gives 0; 0 and 1 gives 0; 0 and 0 gives 0. The result is 1000, which is 8.

12 OR 10. Column by column: 1 or 1 gives 1; 1 or 0 gives 1; 0 or 1 gives 1; 0 or 0 gives 0. The result is 1110, which is 14.

12 XOR 10. Column by column: 1 and 1 are the same, so 0; 1 and 0 differ, so 1; 0 and 1 differ, so 1; 0 and 0 are the same, so 0. The result is 0110, which is 6.

Masking with AND

AND can keep some bits of a number and clear the rest. A mask is a number whose 1 bits mark the positions to keep. The mask 00001111 keeps the four lowest bits.

214 AND 15. 214 is 11010110 and 15 is 00001111. The four high bits meet 0 in the mask, so they become 0. The four low bits stay as they are: 0110. The result is 6.

Flipping bits with XOR

XOR flips a bit wherever the other number has a 1. Where the mask has a 0, the bit is left as it was. Also, any number XOR itself is 0, because every bit matches itself.

5 XOR 7. 5 is 0101 and 7 is 0111. The bits match in the first two places and the last place, so those become 0. The third place differs, so it becomes 1. The result is 0010, which is 2.

Try it

Work these out by hand, then check the answers below.

  1. 6 AND 3
  2. 6 OR 3
  3. 6 XOR 3

Answers: 6 is 0110 and 3 is 0011. AND gives 0010, which is 2. OR gives 0111, which is 7. XOR gives 0101, which is 5.
