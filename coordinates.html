Coordinate grid explained

A coordinate grid locates a point with two numbers. You see pairs like (3, 2) in maths lessons, on maps and in games. The pair tells you where to go from a fixed starting point. This page explains the parts of the grid, how to read a point, how to plot one, and how the four quadrants are named.

The parts of the grid

  • The x-axis is the horizontal line. Numbers on it run left (negative) and right (positive).
  • The y-axis is the vertical line. Numbers on it run down (negative) and up (positive).
  • The origin is where the two axes cross. It is the point (0, 0).

Each axis is a number line, so the ideas on the number lines page apply. Negative numbers on either axis are explained on the negative numbers page.

Reading a point

A point is written as (x, y). The first number is the x-coordinate and the second is the y-coordinate. Always give x first.

  1. Start at the origin (0, 0).
  2. Move along the x-axis by the first number. Right for positive, left for negative.
  3. From there, move up or down by the second number. Up for positive, down for negative.

Example: for (3, 2), move 3 right, then 2 up. For (3, 2) and (2, 3) you end at different places, so the order matters.

Plotting a point

To plot a point, do the same steps and mark where you stop. Plotting (-4, 1) means moving 4 left from the origin, then 1 up, and marking that spot.

The four quadrants

The two axes split the grid into four quarters called quadrants. They are numbered from 1 to 4, starting at the top right and going anticlockwise.

Quadrantx-coordinatey-coordinateExample
I (top right)positivepositive(3, 2)
II (top left)negativepositive(-4, 1)
III (bottom left)negativenegative(-2, -3)
IV (bottom right)positivenegative(5, -2)

A point on an axis is in no quadrant. For example (0, 4) lies on the y-axis and (4, 0) lies on the x-axis. The origin (0, 0) lies on both.

Worked examples

Point (5, -2). Start at the origin. Move 5 right. Move 2 down. The point is in quadrant IV.

Point (-2, -3). Move 2 left and 3 down. Both numbers are negative, so the point is in quadrant III.

Point (-4, 1). Move 4 left and 1 up. The x-coordinate is negative and the y-coordinate is positive, so the point is in quadrant II.

Quick check

  1. Which quadrant is (1, -1) in?
  2. Which quadrant is (-7, -2) in?
  3. Describe the path from the origin to (-3, 4).
  4. Which point is 2 units up from the origin on the y-axis?
Answers
  1. Quadrant IV (x is positive, y is negative).
  2. Quadrant III (both are negative).
  3. Move 3 left, then 4 up.
  4. (0, 2).
