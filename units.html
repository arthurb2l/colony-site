Units and conversion

A unit says what a number measures: 3 kilometres is a length, 3 kilograms is a mass, and 3 litres is a volume. Converting means writing the same amount in a different unit, such as turning 2,500 metres into 2.5 kilometres. This page explains the metric prefixes, shows how to convert length, mass and volume, lists a few common imperial factors for reference, and ends with a quick check.

Metric prefixes

The metric system builds bigger and smaller units by putting a prefix in front of a base unit. Each prefix is a fixed power of ten. The base units on this page are the metre (length), the gram (mass) and the litre (volume).

Two prefixes cover most everyday needs: kilo for large amounts and milli or centi for small ones. The pattern is simple. Each step to the left in the table is a factor of ten.

Some useful facts follow directly from the prefixes:

  • 1 km = 1,000 m, and 1 m = 100 cm, so 1 km = 100,000 cm.
  • 1 m = 1,000 mm.
  • 1 kg = 1,000 g, and 1 g = 1,000 mg.
  • 1 L = 1,000 mL. A volume of 1 cubic centimetre is the same as 1 mL.

How to convert

Ask whether the new unit is smaller or larger than the old one.

  • Smaller unit, bigger number. Going from kilometres to metres, or from litres to millilitres, multiply by the factor.
  • Bigger unit, smaller number. Going from millimetres to metres, or from grams to kilograms, divide by the factor.

Then check that the answer makes sense. A smaller unit gives a bigger number for the same amount: 4 m is 400 cm, a much bigger count than 4.

Check one example by reasoning, not only by the rule. 240 cm is two full metres (200 cm) and 40 cm more, which is 2 and 0.4 metres: 2.4 m. The answer is smaller than 240 because the unit is bigger.

Imperial units for reference

Some visitors meet inch, pound and gallon measures on labels, recipes or road signs. These factors are approximate and rounded for reference:

Imperial unitMetric equivalent
1 inch2.54 cm (exact)
1 foot (12 inches)about 30 cm (30.48 cm)
1 mileabout 1.6 km
1 poundabout 0.45 kg
1 US gallonabout 3.8 L

To estimate a length in inches, multiply the inches by 2.5 for a quick rough value in centimetres. The exact factor is 2.54, so 10 inches is 25.4 cm, close to the estimate of 25 cm.

Try it

  1. Convert 3 km to metres.
  2. Convert 240 cm to metres.
  3. Convert 1,500 g to kilograms.
  4. Convert 0.8 L to millilitres.
  5. Convert 35 mm to centimetres.
Answers

1. 3,000 m (multiply by 1,000). 2. 2.4 m (divide by 100). 3. 1.5 kg (divide by 1,000). 4. 800 mL (multiply by 1,000). 5. 3.5 cm (millimetres to centimetres: divide by 10, since 1 cm = 10 mm).
