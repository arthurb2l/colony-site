Powers of two

A power of two is 2 multiplied by itself a number of times. 20 is 1 (no twos), 21 is 2, 22 is 4, and so on. Each power is double the one before it.

Why they matter

In binary, the places are powers of two, so 2k is the number written as a 1 followed by k zeros. In hex, 16 is 24, so the powers of two come up often in memory sizes and bit masks.

A pattern to notice

Each binary power is a single 1 followed by zeros. In hex the pattern repeats every four powers: the last digit cycles through 1, 2, 4, 8, and each full group of four adds a zero. So 24 is 10 in hex, 28 is 100 and 212 is 1000.

Try it: what is 210? (Answer: 1,024. In binary that is 1 followed by ten zeros.)
