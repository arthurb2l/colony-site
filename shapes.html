Shapes explained: 2D and 3D shapes

A shape is a figure with a boundary. Shapes come in two kinds. Flat shapes are 2D (two-dimensional): they have length and width but no depth, like a sheet of paper. Solid shapes are 3D (three-dimensional): they also have depth, like a box or a ball.

The words for the parts

  • Side: a straight edge of a flat (2D) shape.
  • Vertex (plural vertices): a corner, where two sides or edges meet.
  • Face: a flat surface of a solid (3D) shape.
  • Edge: a line where two faces meet.

A circle has one curved edge and no straight sides, so it has no sides and no vertices.

Regular and irregular shapes

A flat shape with straight sides is called a polygon. A polygon is regular when all its sides are the same length and all its angles are the same size, like a square or an equal-sided triangle. Otherwise it is irregular, like a rectangle that is not a square, or a triangle with three different sides.

A cube has six square faces, all the same size. A cuboid is like a box: its faces are rectangles, and opposite faces are equal. A prism has two identical ends joined by flat faces. A pyramid has one base and triangular faces that meet at one point.

A check that always works for these shapes

For a solid with only flat faces, the number of vertices minus the number of edges plus the number of faces always equals 2:

V − E + F = 2

For a cube: 8 − 12 + 6 = 2. For a square-based pyramid: 5 − 8 + 5 = 2. If your count gives a different answer, you have missed a face, edge or vertex.

Worked example: a triangular prism

  1. Faces: 2 triangles (the two ends) and 3 rectangles (the sides), so F = 5.
  2. Vertices: 3 corners on each triangle, so V = 6.
  3. Edges: 3 on each triangle and 3 joining the two triangles, so E = 9.
  4. Check: 6 − 9 + 5 = 2. The count is correct.

Try it

  1. How many vertices does a pentagon have? (Answer: 5.)
  2. A solid has 6 vertices and 10 edges. How many faces does it have, if it has only flat faces? (Answer: 6, since 6 − 10 + F = 2 gives F = 6.)
  3. Is a rectangle that is not a square regular or irregular? (Answer: irregular, because its sides are not all the same length.)
