Scientific notation explained

Scientific notation writes a number as a product of two parts: a number from 1 up to (but not including) 10, and a power of 10. The general form is a × 10n, where 1 ≤ a < 10 and n is a whole number, which can be negative. It is a short way to write very large or very small numbers.

The power of 10 tells you how far the decimal point moves. 103 is 1,000, so 4.5 × 103 is 4.5 × 1,000 = 4,500.

Large numbers

For a number greater than 10, the exponent is positive. Move the decimal point to the left until one non-zero digit is in front of it. Each move adds 1 to the exponent.

Example: 4,500. Move the decimal point 3 places left to get 4.5. The exponent is 3, so 4,500 = 4.5 × 103.

Example: 60,000,000. Move the decimal point 7 places left to get 6. The exponent is 7, so 60,000,000 = 6 × 107. Check: 107 is 10,000,000, and 6 × 10,000,000 = 60,000,000.

Small numbers

For a number between 0 and 1, the exponent is negative. Move the decimal point to the right until one non-zero digit is in front of it. Each move subtracts 1 from the exponent.

Example: 0.0032. Move the decimal point 3 places right to get 3.2. The exponent is −3, so 0.0032 = 3.2 × 10−3. Check: 3.2 ÷ 1,000 = 0.0032.

A negative exponent means a division by a power of 10. 10−2 is 1 ÷ 100 = 0.01. The negative numbers page explains the sign rules, and the decimals page explains the place values to the right of the point.

Sign of the number

The power of 10 is always positive in front of a negative number; only the first part carries the minus sign. −4,500 = −4.5 × 103.

Multiplying and dividing

Multiply the first parts together, and add the exponents. Divide the first parts, and subtract the exponents. Then fix the first part if it is no longer between 1 and 10.

  • (2 × 103) × (3 × 104) = 6 × 107. Check: 2,000 × 30,000 = 60,000,000.
  • (8 × 106) ÷ (2 × 102) = 4 × 104. Check: 8,000,000 ÷ 200 = 40,000.

When the first part is 10 or more, or below 1, move the decimal point and adjust the exponent. For example, 12 × 103 = 1.2 × 104, because 12,000 = 1.2 × 10,000.

Adding and subtracting

Numbers can be added directly only when the powers of 10 are the same. Rewrite one of them first.

Example: 3 × 104 + 5 × 103. Write 5 × 103 as 0.5 × 104. Then 3 + 0.5 = 3.5, so the sum is 3.5 × 104. Check: 30,000 + 5,000 = 35,000.

Calculators

Many calculators show scientific notation with the letter E, so 4.5E3 means 4.5 × 103. The letter stands for "exponent" and is not a unit. Knowing the form above helps you read it correctly.

Try it

  • Write 72,000 in scientific notation. (Answer: 7.2 × 104.)
  • Write 0.05 in scientific notation. (Answer: 5 × 10−2.)
  • Write 2.5 × 105 as an ordinary number. (Answer: 250,000.)
  • Work out (4 × 102) × (5 × 10−1). (Answer: 2 × 102, which is 200. Check: 400 × 0.5 = 200.)
