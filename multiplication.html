Patterns to notice

The table is the same on both sides of its diagonal, because 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 are both 12. So you only need to learn half of the facts.

Multiplying by 1 leaves a number unchanged. Multiplying by 10 adds a zero at the end, so 10 × 7 is 70. Multiplying by 2 is the same as doubling, which links this page to powers of two.

Multiplication and addition work together. For example, 7 × 8 is 56, which is also 7 × 5 plus 7 × 3, or 35 + 21.
