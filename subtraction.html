Subtraction explained

Subtraction answers one of two questions: how much is left when you take some away, or how far apart two numbers are. Both give the same answer, so you can use whichever picture makes more sense to you.

Two ways to think about it

Taking away. You have 52 apples and give away 18. How many are left? Start with 52 and remove 18, which leaves 34.

Difference. One train is at platform 52 and another at platform 18. How many platforms apart are they? Count the gap between 18 and 52, which is 34.

The words

NameMeaningIn 52 − 18 = 34
Minuendthe number you start with52
Subtrahendthe number you take away18

Counting up on a number line

To work out 52 − 18 in your head, start at the smaller number and count up to the larger one, keeping a running total:

18 --(+2)--> 20 --(+30)--> 50 --(+2)--> 52
total added: 2 + 30 + 2 = 34

Jumping to the nearest ten first (18 to 20) makes the steps easy. The number line page shows this in more detail.

Column subtraction with borrowing

When you write the numbers in columns, subtract one column at a time, starting from the right. If the top digit is smaller than the bottom digit, borrow: exchange one ten from the next column for ten ones.

Example 1: 52 − 18

  5 2
- 1 8
-----

Units: 2 is smaller than 8, so borrow one ten. The 2 becomes 12, and the tens digit drops from 5 to 4. Then 12 − 8 = 4.
Tens: 4 − 1 = 3.
Answer: 34.

Example 2: 305 − 147

  3 0 5
- 1 4 7
-------
  1 5 8

Units: 5 is smaller than 7. The tens digit is 0, so first borrow from the hundreds: the 3 becomes 2 and the 0 becomes 10. Then borrow one ten from that 10, leaving 9 tens and making the units 15. Units: 15 − 7 = 8.
Tens: 9 − 4 = 5.
Hundreds: 2 − 1 = 1.
Answer: 158.

Check your answer by adding

Subtraction and addition undo each other, so you can check any answer by adding the difference back to the number you subtracted. If the sum does not return the starting number, something went wrong.

  • 52 − 18 = 34, and 34 + 18 = 52. ✓
  • 305 − 147 = 158, and 158 + 147 = 305. ✓

The addition page shows the carrying method that this check uses.
